## 💡 Tips for the best results

1. **Specify the tone (optional)** — add a line such as *"Keep the tone professional and suitable for a business email"* or *"Keep the tone casual and friendly for social media."*
2. **Read the Grammar Notes** — forcing Claude to explain its changes makes it double-check its own work, which significantly reduces awkward phrasing and grammatical errors.
3. **Give context for jargon** — if the text is full of technical terms (software engineering, tech, etc.), add a rule: *"Keep specific English terms when there is no direct or commonly used Lao equivalent."*
