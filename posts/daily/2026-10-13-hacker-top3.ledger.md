## STORY 1 (LEAD) — Japan calls a "state of emergency in cyberspace"

### 5W1H
- WHO: Masaaki Taira (head, LDP/National Cybersecurity Strategy HQ); Cybersecurity/DX Minister Toshiharu Furukawa; National Cybersecurity Office; FSA
- WHAT: Emergency meeting; Taira called it "a state of emergency in cyber space"; mandatory hardening instructions to ministries, local governments, companies
- WHEN: 2026-10-09 (JST)
- WHERE: Japan
- WHY NOW: Wave of breaches — 20+ major firms disclosed attacks in recent weeks; IDC Frontier (SoftBank) cloud ransomware 2026-10-07 hit 495 companies and local governments
- HOW: Instructions: update software, strong passwords, supply-chain/contractor security, stronger authentication; FSA asks banks to prefer chip-based ID. Active Cyber Defense "access neutralization" powers live since 2026-10-01
