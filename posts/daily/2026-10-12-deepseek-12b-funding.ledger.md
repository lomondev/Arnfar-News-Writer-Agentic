# Fact ledger — DeepSeek nears ≥80B-yuan (~USD 12B) funding round, IPO prep — 2026-10-12, Asia/Vientiane

Mode: deep (single story).

## 5W1H
- WHO: DeepSeek (Hangzhou AI lab); investors Tencent Holdings and CATL (largest commitments); CITIC Securities (IPO adviser)
- WHAT: Reportedly close to securing at least 80B yuan (~USD 11.93B) in a new round; could approach 100B yuan; restructure afterwards to prepare a domestic IPO
- WHEN: First reported 2026-10-06 (Bloomberg; Reuters same day). Round started July 2026, expected to close in October (Reuters sources)
- WHERE: China; IPO venue under preparation = Shanghai STAR Market
- WHY NOW: Investor demand rose after DeepSeek's latest model launch; China push to reduce reliance on Nvidia (Huawei Ascend partnership)
- HOW: Signed term sheets/commitments above the ~50B-yuan original target; final terms may change before close
