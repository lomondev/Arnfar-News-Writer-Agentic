🚨🤖 In one month, Meta shipped an entire AI lineup: free image generation, plain-language search inside Facebook, and a coding model priced at a quarter of its rivals ⚡️

Hello Arnfar readers! Meta — the company behind Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp — is sprinting to catch up in the AI race. Across June and July 2026 it launched a wave of new models and features, and they land directly on the apps Lao people already open every day: everyday users, online sellers, and developers alike.

🔍 What actually happened

**1) Muse Image — free AI image generation (July 7, 2026)**
Meta unveiled Muse Image, the first image model from Meta Superintelligence Labs.
• Free through the Meta AI app, Instagram Stories and WhatsApp
• Type a prompt to create an image or edit an existing one (erase a photobomber, place yourself in front of a landmark)
• Can generate ad creative and preview furniture in your room via Facebook Marketplace
• According to TechCrunch, Meta says Muse Video is already in development

**2) The privacy backlash**
According to The Verge and TechCrunch, Muse Image lets a user tag someone with a public Instagram account and turn that person's photos into new AI images — and the person is not notified. Meta's setting is on by default; you have to turn it off yourself.

**3) AI Mode on Facebook (June 15, 2026)**
Ask Facebook a question in plain language and Meta AI writes a synthesized answer pulled from public posts, Groups and Reels. Meta also added AI photo editing tools and an AI assistant for creators.

**4) Muse Spark 1.1 — undercutting the competition (July 9, 2026)**
Meta opened the paid API for Muse Spark 1.1, its model for agentic and coding work.
• USD 1.25 per million input tokens, USD 4.25 per million output tokens
• Mark Zuckerberg says that's roughly a quarter of what Anthropic and OpenAI charge (both list USD 5 per million input tokens)
• 1M-token context window, plus USD 20 in free credits for new API accounts

**5) The Iris chip and META stock**
According to Reuters, Meta will begin manufacturing its own AI chip, code-named Iris, in September 2026 (designed with Broadcom, made by TSMC), to grow computing capacity from 7 gigawatts in 2026 to 14 gigawatts in 2027. META shares rose about 6% on Friday, closing the week up 15% — its best week since early 2024.

✅ Pros
✅ Lao users get free ad and product imagery inside apps they already use — no separate software to pay for
✅ Facebook Marketplace sellers can show buyers what an item looks like in their own home
✅ Lao developers cut AI costs sharply: Muse Spark 1.1 is about 4x cheaper than the competition
✅ Fierce competition pushes AI prices down across the whole market — good for everyone

❌ Cons / Watch-outs
❌ Privacy: if your Instagram account is public, others can turn your photos into AI images without you knowing — go into settings and switch it off
❌ AI Mode answers come from ordinary users' posts, not vetted sources, so they can be wrong or out of date
❌ Free, but capped — go past the limit on Muse Image and you'll need a paid plan
❌ Zuckerberg himself told staff on July 2 that AI agent progress over the past four months "hasn't really accelerated in the way that we expected" — keep expectations grounded

📌 Bottom line: Meta is packing AI into every app Lao people use daily while undercutting rivals on price — you gain free tools, but you also need to guard your personal data more carefully.
