# Curated sources — official & specific (for auto-discovery)

Reference list for the "no topic given" flow in `SKILL.md`. These are the
first places to check because they're **primary** (the org's own
announcement) or **specific/high-signal** (a named outlet or writer known for
being first and accurate on this beat) — general `WebSearch` is the
fallback/triangulation step, not the starting point.

Use `WebFetch` on the pages below (or `WebSearch "site:<domain> <keyword>"`
when a page needs date-filtering) to pull the latest headlines, then confirm
anything promising with 1–2 general `WebSearch` queries before drafting —
same accuracy bar as the rest of the skill.

## Stock Trading / Markets

- Nasdaq News — https://www.nasdaq.com/news-and-insights
- NYSE Press Releases — https://www.nyse.com/press-release
- Federal Reserve Press Releases — https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases.htm
- Reuters Markets — https://www.reuters.com/markets/
- Bloomberg Markets — https://www.bloomberg.com/markets

## Notes
- Add to this list over time as new official sources become relevant
  (a new frontier lab, a new exchange, etc.) — keep entries either **official**
  (the subject's own site) or **specific** (a named outlet/writer with a track
  record on that beat), not generic aggregators.
- If a listed page 404s or moves, treat it as stale and fix the URL rather than
  silently dropping the category.
